# Privacy Policy

SuccessfulSuccess is a student project for a university course.

**What we collect.** When you sign in, we receive your email address (and your name, if you
sign in with Google). Your meetings are stored in a database that belongs to this project.

**How we use it.** Only to sign you in and to show you your own meetings. We do not sell or
share this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

**Deletion.** Contact the project owner and your account and data will be deleted.
